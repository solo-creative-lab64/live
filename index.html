How we work

Transparent process from day one.

You'll know exactly what's happening at every stage. No black boxes, no surprises. Here's how every project moves forward.

01
Discovery
Free · 30-minute call

We understand your idea, honestly.

We learn about your product, timeline, and goals. You get a written scope document and an honest assessment — including whether we're the right fit. No sales pitch.

02
Design & Architecture
Week 1–2

Wireframes and technical decisions — before any code.

We design the user experience, database schema, and tech stack. You see everything and approve before development starts. First milestone payment here.

03
Build & Ship
Week 3–8 (varies by scope)

Working software, every week.

You see real, working demos every Friday — not slide decks or status emails. You can test, give feedback, and steer direction in real-time. Second milestone at midpoint.

04
Launch & Handover
Final week

You own everything. We stick around.

Full deployment, documentation, and knowledge transfer. Your repo, your infrastructure, your code — from day one. 30 days of free bug-fix support after launch.

About us

Built on honesty, not hype.

We started BhoomDom because we saw too many founders overpay for software that took too long and didn't work well. Agencies charge for overhead. Large teams burn runway on coordination. The result is the same — late, over-budget, underwhelming.

We're engineers who build production software — the kind of systems that handle real users, real payments, and real scale. We use modern AI tooling to move faster, but every architecture decision, every critical system, and every deployment is deliberate.

We're selective about the projects we take on because we commit fully to each one. If we don't think we're the right fit for what you're building, we'll tell you upfront — before you spend a single dollar.

"We'd rather lose a project by being honest than win one by overpromising."
